# Haven features

What Haven adds on top of Element.

## Social

- **Social app:** A social media view built on Matrix rooms
  ([MSC4501](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4501)). Make a profile, follow
  people, join groups, and read everything in one Feed.
- **Posts:** Post with images and files, then like, repost, quote, reply and react with any emoji.
  You can edit your posts after sending them.
- **Threads:** Replies show as connected chains, the way they do on other social sites.
- **Search:** Find people, groups and posts from Social's Search section.
- **Mentions:** Autocomplete users and rooms while writing a post, and use custom emoji in it.
- **Repost check:** A badge shows whether a repost or quoted post matches the original.
- **Posting permissions:** Choose who can post on your profile or in your group, and let people ask
  to join.
- **External handles:** Show a linked non-Matrix account, such as a Fediverse handle, next to a name
  ([MSC4503](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4503)).
- **Per-message profiles:** Messages sent with their own name and avatar show them, in chat and in
  Social ([MSC4144](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4144)).

## Emoji, stickers and reactions

- **Custom emoji and stickers:** Use image packs from your account and your rooms, with a new picker
  ([MSC2545](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/2545)).
- **Pack management:** Create, edit, copy and delete packs, and add any emoji or sticker you see to
  one of your own.
- **Custom emoji reactions:** React with custom emoji, animated ones included
  ([MSC4027](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4027)).
- **Pack references:** See which pack a custom emoji or sticker came from, and get it for yourself
  ([MSC4459](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4459)).
- **Freeform reactions:** React with any text, not just emoji.
- **Reactions dialog:** See who reacted with what on any message.
- **Reaction size:** Make reaction pills bigger or smaller under Appearance.
- **Emoji style:** Pick a default skin tone, and whether the gun emoji is a revolver, a handgun or a
  water pistol.

## Games and media

- **Games in chat:** Play webxdc games (`.xdc`) right where they're posted, with saves and live
  multiplayer.
- **Flash games:** Play `.swf` games with the built-in Ruffle player.
- **Link embeds:** YouTube, Twitch, X and direct media links play inside the message.
- **HEIC images:** Photos from iPhones show like any other image.
- **Translate links:** Messages in other languages get a "Translate with ..." link to the site you
  choose, or none.

## Apps and widgets

- **Apps:** Social and other apps open from the space bar, with a picker to pin the ones you use.
- **Widgets as apps:** Add a room's widget to your apps and open it full size, without going to the
  room.
- **Games as apps:** Add a game to your apps and play it on a page of its own.
- **Pop-out windows:** Pop an app or a widget out into a window of its own that stays connected.

## Room list

- **Per-section settings:** Each room list section has its own sort order and its own message
  preview setting.
- **Backdrop:** The blurred space avatar behind the room list is back, with a setting for how strong
  it is.
- **Unreads filter:** Choose whether Unreads shows every unread room or only ones that notify you.
- **Old room list:** The previous room list is available as an option.
- **Hide the spaces bar:** Turn off the spaces bar and switch spaces from the room list header.
- **Faster room switching:** Alt+Up/Down skips through rooms without loading each one.

## Rooms and people

- **Banners:** Rooms and spaces can have a banner
  ([MSC4221](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4221)), and so can profiles
  ([MSC4427](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4427)).
- **Device list:** See a user's devices from their profile again, with developer mode on.
- **Mutual rooms:** See which rooms you share with someone from their profile.
- **Room topic:** The room's topic shows under its name in the header.
- **Greentext:** Choose whether lines starting with `>` show as quotes or as greentext.
- **Remove messages:** Remove a user's recent messages, or your own, in one go.
- **Forwarding:** Forward just a file, or just its caption.
- **Encrypted DMs:** Choose whether a new DM is encrypted before sending the first message.
- **Pinned messages banner:** Hide it if you don't want it.

## General

- **Keyboard shortcuts:** Change any keyboard shortcut, and use H/J/K/L to move through lists and
  the emoji picker.
- **Notification sound:** Set one custom notification sound for every room.
